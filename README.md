# SABA Inc. — Enterprise Modeling & Analysis Project

A full enterprise modeling and IT strategy project for a fictional MMORPG game company ("SABA Inc."), done for the Enterprise Modeling & Analysis (EMA) / Information Systems course, MSc Computer and System Science, Stockholm University.

> 🤝 **Group project** — done jointly with Matewos Tesema.

## 📖 What this project covers
Designing a company from the ground up — its business model, goals, processes, and IT architecture — for a startup selling an MMORPG game (physical CDs, streaming via Google Stadia, subscriptions via ISPs, and a third-party in-game equipment marketplace).

### 1. Value Network Analysis (VDML)
Modeled the company's full value network — 9 economic agents (players, retailers, ISPs, Google Stadia, third-party suppliers, Game Forum, CD vendors, ad providers) and the value they exchange with the company (money, games, subscriptions, agreements, innovative ideas).

### 2. Goal Design (BMM)
A goal model connecting the company's vision ("be players' favorite game producer") down through sub-goals, measurable objectives (e.g. 5 million subscribers by end of 2021), and the means to achieve them — cross-referenced against Porter's Five Forces (competitive rivalry, new entrants, substitutes, buyer/supplier power).

### 3. Process Design (REA & EPC)
- **REA ontology diagrams** modeling Sales & Delivery and Purchase & Payment exchange processes — economic agents, resources, and events with full duality relationships
- **EPC (Event-driven Process Chain) diagrams** detailing the Sales Order and Procurement processes step-by-step, including approval/rejection branches and exception handling

### 4. IT Architecture Design
Designed the company's IT system landscape — ETL pipeline, Data Warehouse, BI tools, and an executive dashboard — showing data flow from operational departments (Warehouse, Manufacturing, Procurement) up to company directors.

### 5. Business Performance Management
Defined 5 leading/lagging KPIs (e.g. game subscription growth via ISPs, product advertising reach), each mapped to a specific company goal, business process, and IT data source.

### 6. Enterprise Architecture Evaluation
A capability-based evaluation tying goals, KPIs, and processes together into a cohesive enterprise architecture view.

## 🧰 Tools & methods used
VDML (Value Delivery Modeling Language), BMM (Business Motivation Model), REA ontology, EPC (Event-driven Process Chains), draw.io for diagramming, Porter's Five Forces framework, Eckerson's KPI framework

Hands-on practice designing a company end-to-end from a business-IT alignment perspective — connecting strategic goals to concrete business processes, and those processes to the IT systems and KPIs that support them. Learned how enterprise modeling frameworks (VDML, REA, EPC, BMM) fit together to give a coherent picture of an organization, from value exchange down to dashboard-level metrics.
